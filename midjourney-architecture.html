How to Write Midjourney V6 Prompts for Architecture

Mastering AI for architectural visualization requires specific keywords. In Midjourney V6, natural language works better than a list of random tags.

1. The Core Formula

Use this structure for predictable results: [Style/Era] + [Building Type] + [Setting/Environment] + [Lighting] + [Camera Angles].

2. Best Keywords for Realism

To avoid the "plastic AI look", always include architectural photography terms.

Prompt: A minimalist brutalist concrete villa cliffside overlooking the ocean, dusk, warm interior lighting, photorealistic, architectural photography, shot on 35mm lens, 8k --ar 16:9 --v 6.0

3. Lighting is Everything

Specify the time of day. "Golden hour" creates soft shadows, while "overcast" gives a moody, hyper-realistic Scandinavian vibe.
